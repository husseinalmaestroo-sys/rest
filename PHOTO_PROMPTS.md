# Photo prompts for AI-generated images

Every photo slot on the site, with a ready-to-paste prompt, aspect ratio and file name.
Use one image generator for all of them (for example ChatGPT, Gemini or Midjourney) so the set looks consistent.

**How to use:**
1. Copy the **style line** and paste it at the end of every prompt.
2. Generate each image at the aspect ratio given.
3. Name each file as shown and send them all back in the chat. They'll be wired into `src/content/photos.ts`.

**Style line (add to every prompt):**

> Editorial food photography for an independent Middle Eastern bakery and market. Natural window light, warm tones, soft shadows, shallow depth of field, shot on 35mm film with subtle grain. Worn wood, kraft paper and simple ceramic surfaces. Realistic and appetizing, not glossy. No text, no logos, no signs, no faces.
